# Actions

## What the assistant can do

Besides answering from the documentation, the assistant can look up an order and open a support ticket.
Looking up an order only reads information, so the assistant does it straight away.
Opening a ticket changes something, so it always needs the customer's confirmation first.

## Why it asks you to confirm

When you ask for a ticket, the chat shows a card that lists exactly what will happen, such as the ticket subject and description.
Nothing is done until you press the confirm button on that card.
Typing "yes" in the chat does not count, and neither does anything the assistant says.
You can cancel the card instead, and a request that nobody confirms expires after ten minutes.
Pressing confirm twice never opens two tickets.

## How to track an order

Give the order number, which looks like HD-1042, and the email address on the order.
Both are required.
The assistant gives the same answer when the number does not exist and when the email does not match, so order numbers cannot be guessed.
It reports the order status, the carrier, and the tracking number.

## What it will not do

A chat can open at most three tickets.
Requests such as refunds, cancelling an order, or anything that affects other customers are not something the assistant can do.
